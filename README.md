# 📊 HR Analytics Dashboard

An interactive HR Analytics Dashboard built using Microsoft Power BI to analyze employee data, attrition, demographics, education, job satisfaction, and workforce trends.

## 📌 Project Overview

This project provides a visual analysis of an organization's workforce and helps identify patterns related to employee attrition.

The dashboard allows users to explore employee information based on:

- Education level
- Age group
- Gender
- Department
- Job role
- Job satisfaction
- Employee attrition

## 📊 Dashboard Visualizations

### 1. Department-wise Attrition

Shows the number and percentage of employees who left the organization across different departments:

- R&D
- Sales
- HR

### 2. Number of Employees by Age Group

Analyzes employee distribution across different age groups:

- Under 25
- 25–34
- 35–44
- 45–54
- Above 55

The visualization also provides a gender-wise comparison.

### 3. Job Satisfaction Rating

Displays job satisfaction ratings from 1 to 4 across different job roles, including:

- Healthcare Representative
- Human Resources
- Laboratory Technician
- Manager
- Manufacturing Director
- Research Director
- Research Scientist
- Sales Executive
- Sales Representative

### 4. Education Field-wise Attrition

Analyzes employee attrition based on education field, including:

- Life Sciences
- Medical
- Marketing
- Other
- Technical Degree
- Human Resources

### 5. Attrition Rate by Gender and Age Group

Shows attrition patterns across different age groups and compares male and female employees.

Age groups include:

- Under 25
- 25–34
- 35–44
- 45–54
- Above 55

## 🎯 Objectives

The main objectives of this project are:

- Analyze employee attrition
- Identify departments with higher attrition
- Understand employee demographics
- Analyze job satisfaction
- Study education-wise attrition
- Compare attrition across gender and age groups
- Provide an interactive dashboard for HR decision-making
